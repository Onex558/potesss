What is Meccha Chameleon?

Meccha Chameleon (also written mecha chameleon or mecca chameleon) is a fast, friendly party game built around camouflage. Each round, hiders morph their chameleon's color to match nearby surfaces and disappear into the scenery, while seekers race around the map trying to spot the tiniest movement. It's the classic chameleon game of hide and seek, reimagined for online multiplayer play with friends.

How to play the chameleon hide-and-seek game

  • Hide: change your chameleon's color, cling to walls, and stay still to camouflage.
  • Seek: patrol the map, watch for out-of-place colors, and tag the hidden chameleons.
  • Play online: create or join a room and play Meccha Chameleon with friends in real time.
  • Win: survive as a hider, or find every chameleon as the seeker before time runs out.

Game modes

Every match is a quick round of camouflage and pursuit. Meccha Chameleon has three online modes the room host can choose:

  • 🧟 Infection — when a hider is tagged with paint they instantly join the seekers, so the hunting team grows bigger and bigger until the last chameleon is caught.
  • ⚪ Normal — tagged hiders are knocked out of the round and the seekers win by catching every hidden chameleon before time runs out.
  • ⚔️ Versus — there are no fixed teams: everyone hides during the camouflage phase, then when the timer flips everyone becomes a seeker at once and hunts each other. Tag the most players to top the scoreboard.

Each match flows through a lobby, a countdown, a hide & camouflage phase, and a hunt phase, before the results screen.

Controls — how to play on mobile & PC

Meccha Chameleon is built for touch and keyboard, so you can play anywhere:

  • Mobile — hiding: swipe to look around, tap a surface to paint your camouflage color, and tap a wall to soak up its color. Use the rotate button to face the wall and cling like a real chameleon.
  • Mobile — seeking: use the joystick to move and hold the fire button to shoot paint while you swipe to aim.
  • Desktop — hiding: WASD to move, drag the mouse to look, left-click to paint, right-click to change pose, scroll to zoom.
  • Desktop — seeking: WASD to move, click to lock the mouse and aim, left-click or F to fire, Esc to release.
  • Signature moves: double-jump, then cling to walls and climb across ceilings to reach hiding spots seekers won’t think to check.
